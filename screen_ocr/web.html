Click a headword to copy it; a blue tick after the time means you’ve copied it before. Click the box in the margin to select, Shift-click to select a range. Right-click a tab, or use its ⋯ button, to rename or remove a category. An entry can carry up to three labels of your own, like “lesson 18”; click one to see only those entries. Search looks in the text, categories and labels, and you can type Japanese, Chinese or Korean in Latin letters (“tokyo”, “mingtian”, “annyeong”). Everything stays on this computer.
